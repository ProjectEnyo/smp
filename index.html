Server Information

The Survival project is set up to provide classic Minecraft Survival with PVP from the early days of Minecraft multiplayer. Simply, it means you play Minecraft as it was meant to be played; build great things together, make friends… or enemies.

You are safe from PVP if you stick to the safe zone, and you may build freely there as griefing is not allowed. Leaving for the wilderness means you can get killed, and your base might get looted by other players.

PVP might not be for everyone, in which case you can stick to the safe zone. You will be in a limited area with limited resources and may need to wander into the wilderness at some point.

Many people enjoy the potential danger brought on by building in such a hostile environment without ever actually starting a fight, and we welcome anyone who chooses to play this way.

~ Nerd.nu Survival, 2010–2014

Features

Certain aspects of gameplay are altered from standard Minecraft;

  • Combat tagging
  • Limited map size
  • Structures are not generated
  • The End is not available
  • No Crystal or Totem PVP
  • No Respawn Anchor
  • No Ender Chests
  • Skipping the night is disabled
